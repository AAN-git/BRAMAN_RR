Spectre Series II

A Triumph of Precision Engineering

Spectre Series II redefines the super coupé — its uncompromising expression of elegance and agility elevates contemporary luxury to an art form.

Spectre positions Rolls-Royce’s entrance into the electric world as a stamp of leadership — and the beginning of an era that will see the entire model portfolio electrified by 2030. Its visionary design reinvents timeless iconography, setting a clear path toward the marque’s first all-electric decade.

Available to Test Drive and Order. Contact our team at Rolls-Royce Motor Cars Palm Beach today for more information.

Interior

Timeless Design

Inside Spectre Series II, a collection of exceptional features reflects the motor car’s contemporary character.

Superior seating

Spectre Series II’s seat design wraps itself around the passenger, cocooning them in supple leather.

Enchanting details

Elevating Spectre Series II, Placed Perforation leather unveils hidden artworks through precise patterns. Breathing a delicate artistry into the interior, designers have captured the shifting silhouettes cast by clouds in moonlight.

A cascade of light

Spectre Series II debuts a new Illuminated Fascia graphic, its design inspired by the shifting mist that settles over the woodlands near the Home of Rolls-Royce at Goodwood.

A celestial entrance

A spellbinding option for the interior, Starlight Doors radiate with 4,796 backlit perforations. Each ‘star’ is positioned by hand, creating a subtle sense of motion within the cabin.

A Modern Masterpiece
