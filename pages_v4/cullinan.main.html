Cullinan Series II

Journey in Elegance

Confident and sophisticated. Commanding, yet refined. In the comfort of exquisite craftsmanship, individuals of excellence uncover true freedom. Experience the epitome of effortless everywhere.

A powerful presence with endless capabilities, Cullinan belongs to the invincible.

Exterior

Commanding Contours

Vertical headlights and daytime running lights borrow inspiration from the towering skyscrapers of urban landscapes. The iconic Illuminated Grille — seen for the first time on Cullinan — is refreshed with a lower leading edge and a geometric ‘horizon line’.

Impressions of Authority

Simple feature lines and clean, monolithic surfaces amplify the commanding presence of Cullinan. When adorned in Emperador Truffle — a contemporary, minimalist solid grey-brown — the flawless surfaces exude pure sophistication.

Interior

Inspired Craftsmanship

Fine materials and contemporary craft techniques combine, creating works of art throughout the interior.

Consisting of up to 107,000 0.8 and 1.2-millimetre perforations, the supple leather seats showcase a Bespoke Placed Perforation design inspired by the shapes and shadows of clouds swirling above the Home of Rolls-Royce in Goodwood.

Urban Brilliance

The Illuminated Fascia reveals a brilliant wordmark set against a vertical motif. These illuminations — crafted from 7,000 graphical elements laser-etched on glass — replicate a glittering metropolis after dark.

With the advanced SPIRIT operating system, Bespoke personalisation now extends from the physical to the digital realm. Instrument dials are colour-customised, seamlessly harmonising with the interior or exterior palette.

Formidable in presence. Extraordinary in craftsmanship. Experience the freedom to go anywhere, effortlessly, with this enhanced icon.
