Ghost Series II

Advanced Power

With Ghost, life becomes amplified. Its exhilarating performance and modern presence captivate those who embrace every moment. As the most technologically advanced V12 powered Rolls-Royce, every drive with Ghost is unforgettable.

A distinguished masterpiece designed to exhilarate.

Exterior

A Flawless Vision

A distilled, monolithic front profile draws attention to the iconic Illuminated Grille, which emits a divine glow on command. Beneath, the lower front end is accentuated with chrome embellishments, creating a ‘floating’ visual for the Spirit of Ecstasy.

A Heightened Experience

Ghost strikes a perfect balance between potent power and enticing Bespoke craftsmanship. Every drive and detail is a testament to excellence, thrilling the senses of those behind the wheel.

Interior

Radiant Artistry

The Illuminated Fascia comes to life with a cosmic design inspired by time-lapse celestial photography. This brilliant artwork is framed by Grey Stained Ash — a richly grained, natural open-pore wood.

Tailored Textile

The seats are adorned with Duality Twill, a new rayon fabric crafted from bamboo, inspired by an extensive bamboo grove at Le Jardin Méditerranées on the Côte d’Azur — the neighbour to Sir Henry Royce’s former winter home, Villa Mimosa. The fabric took over a year to develop in collaboration with a single master weaver. A complete Duality Twill interior requires up to 2.2 million stitches and 11 miles of thread, meticulously crafted over 20 hours.

The Drive

Self-Assured Confidence

Experience the world’s most exclusive destinations with power, control, and agility.

A silken drive

The renowned Planar Suspension System incorporates a unique Upper Wishbone Damper that operates alongside shock absorbers and self-levelling, high-volume air strut assemblies. This ensures a smooth, stable, and effortless Magic Carpet Ride sensation for driver and passengers.

Superior response

Wherever you drive, Ghost anticipates and reacts with ease. The Flagbearer system reads the road ahead, preparing the suspension for changes in road surface.

Refined power

The all-wheel steering, all-wheel drivetrain is powered by the 6.75-litre twin-turbocharged V12, delivering maximum torque from just 1,600rpm.
