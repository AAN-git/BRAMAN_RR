01Rules that hold everywhere

From the client's notes and the design reviews. Every page in version 4 already keeps them; anything new should too.

  • A calm pageAn older clientele: a static page you can read. Inventory, Specials, Finance and Contact Us are always on screen: in the bar on a wide screen, in a bar at the foot below 1024px.
  • One weightRiviera Nights Regular only. Version 4 has no Light: the Light face is re-pointed at the Regular file.
  • Type is Frost or NoirNoir on the light grounds, Frost on the dark ones and on photographs. The quieter registers are tints of the same colour.
  • 14px floorNo functional text under 14px. On a phone, body copy is 18px.
  • Capitals stay shortHeadings, labels and buttons may be capitals; running text never is.
  • No words on a busy photographCopy stands on a ground, or on a part of the photograph kept quiet for it.
  • Phone keeps the boxesWhat is boxed on a desktop is boxed on a phone. Buttons go full width. No holes between or inside sections.
  • Noir is rareThe brand keeps Noir for Black Badge. Elsewhere the coast: Frost, Pebble, Sky, Pearl. Purple Spirit closes the page.

08Forms

On a light plate, fields are ruled lines, not filled boxes: a Noir 28% line under each, darker on hover, Noir on focus. The message keeps a hairline frame. Labels are 14px capitals.

09Motion

Quiet by default. Lenis smooths the scroll on every version-4 page. On the home page from 768px, Cullinan and Black Badge hold full-screen while their words leave, scrubbed to the scroll (GSAP, ScrollTrigger, SplitText). Everything is off under prefers-reduced-motion; a phone keeps the plain page.
